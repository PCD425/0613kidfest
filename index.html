Now imagine a tool designed for moments when a child’s familiar route changes.

Kidova is not just a location tracker. It combines a child’s familiar journey with city and street-level information — such as transit changes, road conditions, risky crossings, weather, or nearby safe places — to help grown-ups understand what is happening around the child. Instead of only seeing where the child is, the grown-up can better understand the situation and decide how to support them.
Privacy promise: This event prototype does not ask for a child's name, school, home address, or live location.
